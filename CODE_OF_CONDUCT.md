# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want IssueHarbor to be a welcoming, respectful project for people with different backgrounds and levels of experience.

## Expected behavior

- Be considerate and constructive.
- Discuss ideas, not personal traits.
- Respect differing viewpoints and accept clear, actionable feedback.
- Keep shared project spaces free from harassment, insults, and unwanted attention.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, sexualized attention without consent, and persistent disruptive conduct are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately through GitHub's contact options for the repository owner. Reports will be reviewed and handled as fairly and confidentially as possible. Project maintainers may remove content or restrict participation when needed to protect the community.
